# Utubee Domain Glossary

The domain model and ubiquitous language for the Utubee-Thumbnail & Study Companion browser extension, defining how students consume academic video content intentionally.

## Language

### Visual Integrity

**Authentic Frame**:
A video still extracted directly from the video stream at a deterministic runtime milestone, free of post-production graphics.
_Avoid_: Middle picture, honest thumbnail, snapshot.

**Creator Thumbnail**:
The custom image uploaded by the video publisher to market the video across discovery feeds.
_Avoid_: Original thumbnail, natural thumbnail, clickbait graphic.

**Frame Milestone**:
A standardized percentage offset within a video's runtime (25% start, 50% midpoint, or 75% end) used to sample an authentic frame.
_Avoid_: Timestamp marker, frame position, preview cut.

### Focus & Discovery

**Clean Slate Homepage**:
A minimal, query-first view of YouTube that suppresses algorithmic recommendation feeds and presents only the search interface.
_Avoid_: Blank homepage, search-only mode, distraction-free home.

**Study Shield**:
A declarative visibility rule that eliminates non-essential algorithmic distractions from the student's field of view.
_Avoid_: Blocker, element hider, filter.

**Tunnel-Vision Watch**:
A focused playback mode that suppresses peripheral sidebar recommendations while preserving structured course playlists.
_Avoid_: Theater mode, sidebar blocker, focus watch.

**Course Playlist**:
An ordered sequence of educational videos grouped by an instructor that auto-advances through a syllabus.
_Avoid_: Recommendation list, sidebar playlist, watch queue.

**Shorts Eradication**:
The complete suppression of short-form vertical video shelves and links across all YouTube views to safeguard deep attention.
_Avoid_: Shorts blocker, reel remover, reel filter.

**Errata Panel**:
The primary pinned commentary beneath an academic video containing instructor corrections, code links, and lecture timestamps.
_Avoid_: Comment section, author notes, user discussion.

### Comprehension & Notes

**Slide Capture**:
A lossless, native-resolution visual extraction of the current lecture frame directly to the system clipboard.
_Avoid_: Screenshot, screen grab, snipping tool capture.

**A-B Concept Loop**:
A bounded playback window defined by two timestamps that repeats continuously for concept reinforcement.
_Avoid_: Video repeater, micro-looper, repeat segment.

**Study HUD**:
An ephemeral, non-obtrusive visual overlay on the video player that confirms student actions (loop markers, capture feedback) without blocking learning content.
_Avoid_: Toast notification, alert banner, status popup.
